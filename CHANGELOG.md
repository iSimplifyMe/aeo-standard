# Changelog — The AEO Standard

## 1.0 — July 2026

Initial public release. Seven sections totaling 100 points (Substance &
Originality 25 · Atomic Answer Blocks 15 · Structured Data & Schema 10 ·
RAG/Retrieval Readiness 20 · Semantic HTML & Headings 10 · Internal Linking
& Fan-Out 10 · SEO Meta & Technical 10), two gating rules (hidden
machine-only content; fabricated authority), verdict thresholds at
95/85/70, and mechanical/judgment markers on every check.

Prior to publication the rubric was developed and applied internally
across a seventeen-site production network (2025–2026).
